QUERY 01 // MOBILIZATION schedule

What is the typical lead time for mobilizing to a construction site?

For sites situated within metropolitan Lagos (Mainland, Ikeja, Lekki corridor, Victoria Island), mobilization for urgent concrete pour audits or critical milestone inspections can be executed within 24 to 48 hours following drawing review. Regional mobilizations across Ogun and southwestern Nigeria typically require 3 to 5 business days advance schedule integration.

QUERY 02 // THIRD-PARTY AUDIT verified_user

Can Adeyemi provide independent third-party QA/QC audits for active contractor projects?

Yes. Adeyemi regularly acts on behalf of institutional project owners, client quantity surveyors, and project developers to provide impartial site oversight. This encompasses assessing main contractor reinforcement bar placements against approved drawings, checking concrete testing records, logging non-conformance defects, and ensuring compliance prior to client sign-off.

QUERY 03 // STANDARDS CODES rule

What technical standards govern your inspection reports and testing witnessing?

Inspections and quality assessments are executed in accordance with Eurocode 2 (BS EN 1992-1-1) for reinforced concrete structures, alongside legacy BS 8110 where mandated by tender documents. Earthwork compaction and soils follow BS 1377 standards. All field reports are stamped and referenced to COREN professional engineering practice guidelines.

QUERY 04 // TENDER DOCUMENTATION badge

Are CV credentials and verified academic transcripts available for institutional tendering?

Comprehensive verified technical dossiers—including certified true copies of the COREN Practicing License, Nigerian Society of Engineers (NSE) corporate membership, verified university civil engineering transcripts, and project completion certificates—are provided directly to procurement boards upon submission of formal tender requirements.
